# Question 3 — High-Traffic Product Catalog 🔥

## You're building an e-commerce product catalog.

### Requirements:

- 10 million products
- Millions of users browse products.
- Product reads are extremely high.
- Product information doesn't change frequently.
- 95% of requests are reads.
- The database should not be hit for every request.
- Product updates should eventually become visible to users.
- The system should handle traffic spikes.
- The application should be highly available.
- You need fast response times.
